I live in Spain and work in Madrid as a Full Stack developer, leading software development projects. I've been at the keyboard for more than 25 years, and my interests revolve around software architecture, .NET, Angular, TypeScript, JavaScript, HTML and CSS. Lately I'm especially interested in AI applied to software development and in automation.

I like art, design, the scientific explanation of everything and of why we are the way we are, technology in our lives and web development.

I'm curious by nature, self-taught by conviction and an eternal learner. On this blog I share what I learn along the way.
